# Launch sky

`sky-day.webp` is an original generated background for the local launch UI, created on
2026-10-24 with the built-in Imagegen tool and converted to WebP with cwebp (quality 85).
It adapts the painted daylight sky described in the supplied Clio Design Principles.
It is decorative and never substitutes for passthrough or captured camera imagery.

Prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide background texture for a calm AR learning app launch screen. A quiet hand-painted daylight sky with soft cumulus clouds, cool pale blue #A2CEE3, blue-white slate #EDF2F6, delicate painterly brushwork. Wide landscape composition, open pale quiet center and lower half for dark UI text, softly sculpted clouds around the upper left and right edges. Bottom portion becomes a very pale nearly uniform blue-white atmospheric haze. Refined airy gouache/oil painting, low contrast, natural light. Sky only. No horizon, landscape, objects, text, logos, graphic symbols, panels or UI. Opaque background.
